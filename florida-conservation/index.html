    What's counted

    The footprint is the Florida Natural Areas Inventory's Florida Conservation Lands layer, the state's standard list of managed conservation areas. Government-owned means fee title held by a federal, state, water management district or local agency. Easements are privately owned land where an agency or land trust holds the development rights.

    Florida's land area of 34.6 million acres is the denominator. Park boundaries such as Everglades and Biscayne include open water, so the share runs higher than a land-only count would.

    How each acre is dated

    Each piece of the footprint takes the earliest date from parcel records that cover it: deed dates in DEP's state-owned lands inventory (FL‑SOLARIS), closing dates of Florida Forever acquisitions, and establishment dates in USGS PAD‑US 4.1, which carries the National Conservation Easement Database. Where no parcel record exists, the managed area's establishment year is used and marked as estimated.
